{{ val(business.legal_name, 'registered business name') }} ("NTS") runs this site. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Checkout: your name, email address and mobile number. Guest orders are linked to your email address.
  • Account (optional): if you sign in, your login details. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Orders: delivery addresses, the items you bought, and your payment status. Phone numbers and addresses saved to an account are encrypted in our database; order records keep the delivery details needed to ship and support the order.
  • Cart: saved in your browser, and against your email address once you enter it at checkout.
  • On your device: a session cookie that keeps track of your cart, checkout and login.

We do not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank login details. Razorpay handles all of these.

Why we use it

We use your data to process and deliver your orders, let you look up their status, handle cancellations, returns and refunds, and reply when you contact us. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay: processes your payments and refunds.
  • Shiprocket and its courier partners: receive your name, address and phone number so they can deliver your order and collect returns.
  • Twilio: sends one-time passwords (OTPs) by SMS when phone login is used.
  • Our hosting and database providers, who store the data on our behalf.
  • Government authorities, but only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. We keep order and invoice records for the period tax law requires.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details in Account. To get a copy of your data, have it erased, or withdraw your consent, write to {{ val(business.email, 'support email') }}.
