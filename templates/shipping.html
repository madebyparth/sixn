Where we ship

We ship within India only. At checkout we check that your PIN code is serviceable before you pay.

Charges

  • Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,000 or more.
  • Orders under ₹1,000 pay the courier rate for your PIN code, which we show at checkout before you pay.
  • All prices include GST.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive within 5–7 business days of being placed. Remote PIN codes can take longer. Once your order ships, we add the courier tracking number (AWB) to your order.

Tracking

Track your order from Orders, or with your order number on Order Status.

Cash on delivery

Available on serviceable PIN codes. Please keep the exact amount ready.
