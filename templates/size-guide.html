How to Measure

01. Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.

02. Shoulder

Measure from one shoulder tip to the other across the natural curve of your back.

03. Length

Measure from the top of your shoulder (at the base of your neck) down to your waist.

Between Sizes?

If your measurements are between two sizes, we recommend selecting the larger size for a more comfortable fit, or the smaller size for a slimmer look.
