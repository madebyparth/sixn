Cancelling an order

To cancel an order that has not been delivered, email us with your order number (signed-in customers can also cancel from Orders). If it has already been handed to the courier, we cancel the shipment with the courier.

Returns

  • You can request a return within {{ business.return_days }} days of delivery by emailing us your order number (signed-in customers can use Orders).
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their original tags.
  • We review every request. Once it is approved, a courier collects the item from your delivery address at no cost to you.
  • We do not offer direct size exchanges. Return the item and place a new order in the size you need.

Refunds

  • Paid online (UPI, card, netbanking): refunded to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5–7 business days to show it.
  • Paid partly with NTS wallet credit: the full amount goes back to your NTS wallet straight away, and you can use it on your next order.
  • Cash on delivery: refunded to your UPI ID or bank account (for signed-in customers, as NTS wallet credit).
  • For returns, we issue the refund after the returned item reaches us and passes inspection.

Damaged or wrong item

Write to us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos, and we will arrange a free replacement or a full refund.
