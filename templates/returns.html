How it Works

01.

Visit Order History: Log in to your account and go to the "My Orders" section.

02.

Select Return/Exchange: Click on the order you wish to return and select the product.

03.

Reverse Pickup: Our courier partner will arrive within 24-48 hours to collect the package.

04.

Validation: Once we receive the item and verify its condition, your refund/exchange is processed.

Return Policy Details

  • The item must be in its original condition, unworn, and unwashed.
  • All tags and original packaging must be intact.
  • Items on "Clearance Sale" are only eligible for exchange, not full refunds.
  • For hygiene reasons, certain accessories cannot be returned.
