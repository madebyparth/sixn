How do I pay?

With UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps), a debit or credit card, netbanking, or cash on delivery. Online payments go through Razorpay.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet. We only deliver to PIN codes in India.

How much is shipping and how long does it take?

Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,000 or more, and most orders arrive within 5–7 business days. See Shipping & Delivery.

Which size should I pick?

Our tees come in S, M and L. Measurements are in the size guide.
